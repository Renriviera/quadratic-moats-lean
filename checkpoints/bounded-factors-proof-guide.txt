Bounded distinct prime-ideal factors: proof guide

Statement
For a number field K of degree two, a natural number b, and a real D,
consider the graph on nonzero elements alpha of the ring of integers with
at most b distinct prime ideals dividing (alpha). Edges use the full
Minkowski Euclidean distance and step bound D. There is a uniform bound
on the cardinality of every connected component. Both real and imaginary
quadratic fields are covered; no unique factorization assumption is used.

What is counted
Model.lean defines omega by normalizedFactors of the principal ideal,
followed by toFinset.card. Thus multiplicities disappear. Units have count
zero. Zero is explicitly excluded from Vertex, since Lean's factor list
of zero is empty. The finite periodic sieve itself does not exclude zero.

Changes from the irreducible proof
1. SoftLists and SoftInformation replace a no-hit passing list with the
   candidates hit at most h times among n independent packages. A false
   candidate with posterior hit probability at least c/4 survives with
   probability at most exp(-n*c/32), provided h <= n*c/8.
2. A variable-size list entropy bound pays log 2 plus the missing-true-value
   probability times log alphabet size. Markov bounds this latter loss by
   n/h times the one-package true-hit probability. Empty lists are safe
   because the size term is log(max 1, list cardinality).
3. SoftWalkPackage transports true hits to actual divisibility, under the
   same terminal smoothing law for every batch. Charge all available
   factors in a batch so the charge is independent of entropy selection.
4. For primes p in [T,2T], choose h = log(2T). The original repetition
   count already satisfies the threshold restriction. Since log p <= h,
   the divisor-charge coefficient is at most one. The extra log 2 and
   terminal-shift errors fit the original positive rate at X >= 10000.
5. HitBudget sums these charges across pairwise-disjoint batches. For
   every sampled vertex, there are at most b charged factors in total.
   The telescoping contradiction therefore pays b once, rather than once
   per scale. Main chooses enough windows to exceed initial entropy + b.
6. FactorSupport injects pairwise coprime selected prime generators into
   distinct prime ideals of (alpha). The finite hit-budget sieve is
   periodic, contains every Vertex K b, and has no infinite simple walk.
   Existing lattice periodicity then gives a uniform finite component bound.

Reuse
The original planar geometry, short-displacement norm estimate, coverage
derivation, prime supply, entropy telescope, and periodic component theorem
are imported unchanged. New files live in QuadraticMoat/BoundedFactors/.

Scope limits
This formalization proves the qualitative theorem for degree exactly two.
It does not supply an explicit numerical bound in b, arbitrary forbidden
residue phases, or a higher-degree number-field theorem.

Validation
Run ./lakew build and ./lakew env lean checkpoints/AuditBoundedFactors.lean.
The audit displays the final quantified statement and its axiom dependencies.
